# Descriptive estimands

All released results are survey-weighted descriptive quantities, not causal estimands.

* **Arrangement composition:** a weighted ratio of recorded worker-crop arrangements by labour-arrangement category to all eligible arrangements.
* **Task incidence:** a weighted ratio of arrangements with a specified task-and-arrangement flag to all eligible arrangements. Categories overlap.
* **Arrangement-level hours share:** a weighted ratio of valid recorded arrangement-level person-hours in a category to valid recorded arrangement-level hours. It is not an individual-worker time, productivity, or task-time measure.
* **Demographic and geographic descriptions:** restricted to records for which the source instrument supports the corresponding classification.

Household-level numerator and denominator contributions are constructed before estimation. Confidence intervals are survey-linearised 95% normal-approximation intervals. The final eligible analytic population has 36,200 worker-crop arrangements represented in 71,962 task-expanded records.
