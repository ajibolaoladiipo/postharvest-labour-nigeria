# Limitations and safe interpretation

* A worker-crop arrangement is not a unique worker. External arrangements may aggregate multiple people.
* Task expansion repeats arrangement-level hours. The scripts therefore de-duplicate to the arrangement before all hours aggregation; task-specific time is not observed.
* Wave 4 retains 358 unresolved external records with missing screeners/no follow-up and 16 affirmative unpaid/exchange screeners lacking a worker count. They are not recoded as nonparticipation.
* Wave 4 covers accessible areas as documented by the original survey materials.
* Payment classifications do not establish wage levels, compensation inequality, bargaining, exploitation, or household welfare.
* The current local Wave 5 source matches the published v02 schema/case metadata, but the original historical acquisition-date manifest was not retained.
